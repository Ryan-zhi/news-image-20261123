A one-image summary of the world's news of **Monday 23 November 2026**, drawn
as a newspaper broadsheet: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, the COP31
climate talks collapse, Democrats win their biggest House majority since
1992, Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya, and the U.S. is named a
co-host of the 2031 Women's World Cup — plus an "also today" strip.

`entry.png` is the picture. `main.ts` copies it to the grader's output
directory. The poster itself is drawn by `draw.py` from the story data in
`day23.py`, written from that day's wire reports.
